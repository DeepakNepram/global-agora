# Data sources

Generated by `scripts/places/build.ts` (`npm run places:build`). Do not edit
the generated files by hand.

## places.v1.json

The gazetteer behind place search, place follows and the onboarding's home
city. Columnar JSON: about 290 KB raw, 101 KB gzip, 85 KB Brotli. Fetched the
first time search or the home-city step needs it, never at start.

All of it is from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), which is
**public domain**: "No permission is needed to use Natural Earth. Crediting
the authors is unnecessary." Credit is given here as a courtesy.

| Layer | Used for | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ne_10m_populated_places_simple` (v5) | 7,342 cities: name, ASCII name, country, point, population | [GitHub mirror](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson) |
| `ne_50m_admin_0_countries` (v5) | Countries: names, label point, extent | [GitHub mirror](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson) |

### What the build changes

- Countries are keyed by `ISO_A2_EH`, because `ISO_A2` is `-99` for France and
  Norway. Australia's two features are merged into the larger one.
- Seven territories that the 1:50m layer folds into their country (French
  Guiana, Réunion, Gibraltar and others) get their own name, so their cities'
  labels read correctly.
- The ten cities with no ISO code (in Somaliland and Northern Cyprus) keep their
  country's name.
- Coordinates are rounded to hundredths of a degree (about 1 km), and
  populations to two significant figures. Populations only rank search
  results.
- A country's `radius` is how far it reaches from its label point, counting
  its parts nearest first until 80 % of its area is in. Alaska therefore does
  not widen the United States, nor French Guiana France.
